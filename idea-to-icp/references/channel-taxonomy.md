# Channel Taxonomy Reference

Complete channel list with motion, tags, and best-for guidance. Used in Phase 5 to match channels to the beachhead ICP segment.

## How to Use This File

1. Look at the beachhead segment's `watering_holes` — these are direct channel matches
2. Cross-reference with `market_type` and `buying_model` to filter appropriately
3. Recommend 2-3 channels max — focus beats breadth at early stages
4. For each recommended channel, include a concrete first experiment idea

---

## Outbound Channels

### cold-email
- **Motion:** Outbound
- **Tags:** email, direct
- **Best for:** B2B_SMB, B2B_Mid, SalesAssisted/SalesLed
- **Not great for:** B2C, low-price SelfServe
- **First experiment:** 50 personalized emails to [ICP] found via [source], measuring reply rate

### linkedin-dm
- **Motion:** Outbound
- **Tags:** social, direct, professional
- **Best for:** B2B_SMB, B2B_Mid, Prosumer (professional context)
- **Not great for:** B2C, non-professional audiences
- **First experiment:** 30 connection requests + value-first messages to [ICP title] at [company type]

### twitter-dm
- **Motion:** Outbound
- **Tags:** social, direct
- **Best for:** Prosumer, B2C (tech-savvy), indie hackers, creators
- **Not great for:** B2B_Enterprise, non-tech audiences
- **First experiment:** Engage with 20 [ICP]'s tweets for a week, then DM with relevant offer

### community-participation
- **Motion:** Outbound
- **Tags:** community, organic
- **Best for:** All market types where ICP has identifiable watering holes
- **Not great for:** When no relevant communities exist
- **First experiment:** Spend 2 weeks giving value in [specific community], then soft-mention product in context

### warm-intro
- **Motion:** Outbound
- **Tags:** network, referral
- **Best for:** B2B (all sizes), high-trust purchases
- **Not great for:** Scale (doesn't scale), B2C mass market
- **First experiment:** List 10 people in your network who know [ICP], ask for 3 intros each

## Inbound — Owned

### landing-page
- **Motion:** Inbound
- **Tags:** owned, website, conversion
- **Best for:** All market types — foundational, always needed
- **Not great for:** N/A (every product needs one)
- **First experiment:** Single page with headline, pain statement, CTA. Drive 100 visitors from one source, measure conversion

### seo-content
- **Motion:** Inbound
- **Tags:** owned, organic, content
- **Best for:** Prosumer, B2B_SMB, products solving searchable problems
- **Not great for:** Brand-new categories (no search volume), urgent needs
- **First experiment:** Write 3 articles targeting "[ICP pain] + solution" keywords, publish, measure organic traffic after 30 days

### blog
- **Motion:** Inbound
- **Tags:** owned, content
- **Best for:** Thought leadership, Prosumer, B2B with considered purchases
- **Not great for:** Impulse-buy B2C, products where audience doesn't read
- **First experiment:** Publish weekly for 4 weeks on topics from ICP's watering holes, measure email signups

### newsletter
- **Motion:** Inbound
- **Tags:** owned, email, nurture
- **Best for:** All market types as a nurture layer
- **Not great for:** As a primary acquisition channel (need traffic first)
- **First experiment:** Set up welcome sequence (3 emails), drive 50 signups from primary channel, measure open rates

## Inbound — Paid

### google-ads
- **Motion:** Inbound
- **Tags:** paid, search, intent
- **Best for:** Products solving searchable problems, Mid+ budget bands, SelfServe
- **Not great for:** New categories, Micro budget products (unit economics don't work)
- **First experiment:** $200 budget, 10 exact-match keywords around "[ICP pain]", measure CPC and conversion

### meta-ads
- **Motion:** Inbound
- **Tags:** paid, social, awareness
- **Best for:** B2C, Prosumer, visual products, broad audiences
- **Not great for:** B2B_Enterprise, niche professional audiences
- **First experiment:** $100 budget, 3 ad variations targeting [ICP demographics], measure CPL

### newsletter-sponsorship
- **Motion:** Inbound
- **Tags:** paid, sponsorship, targeted
- **Best for:** Niche audiences with dedicated newsletters, Prosumer, B2B_SMB
- **Not great for:** Mass market B2C (expensive per impression)
- **First experiment:** Sponsor 1 issue of [newsletter that ICP reads], unique link, measure clicks and signups

## Inbound — Earned

### product-hunt
- **Motion:** Inbound
- **Tags:** earned, launch, one-time
- **Best for:** SaaS, developer tools, Prosumer, indie products
- **Not great for:** B2B_Enterprise, local businesses, non-tech audiences
- **First experiment:** Prepare launch page, recruit 20 supporters, launch on Tuesday, measure signups for 48 hours

### indie-hackers
- **Motion:** Inbound
- **Tags:** earned, community, builders
- **Best for:** SaaS, developer tools, products for indie builders
- **Not great for:** B2C consumer, non-tech audiences
- **First experiment:** Share build-in-public story, link to waitlist, measure signups

### reddit
- **Motion:** Inbound
- **Tags:** earned, community, niche
- **Best for:** Any niche with an active subreddit
- **Not great for:** Self-promotion (Reddit hates it), broad products
- **First experiment:** Identify 3 subreddits where ICP asks questions you solve, provide value for 2 weeks, then share product naturally

### micro-influencer
- **Motion:** Inbound
- **Tags:** earned, influencer, social-proof
- **Best for:** B2C, Prosumer, visual/lifestyle products
- **Not great for:** B2B_Enterprise, deeply technical products
- **First experiment:** Identify 5 micro-influencers (1K-50K followers) in [ICP's space], offer free access, measure referral signups

### word-of-mouth
- **Motion:** Inbound
- **Tags:** earned, referral, organic
- **Best for:** Products with strong "aha moment", all market types
- **Not great for:** As a primary strategy pre-PMF (too slow)
- **First experiment:** Add "refer a friend" mechanism, offer incentive, measure viral coefficient over 30 days

### podcast-guesting
- **Motion:** Inbound
- **Tags:** earned, content, authority
- **Best for:** B2B, Prosumer, founder-led brands
- **Not great for:** B2C mass market, products that need visual demo
- **First experiment:** Pitch 5 podcasts that [ICP] listens to, book 1 appearance, measure traffic spike and signups

## Channel Selection Matrix

Quick reference for matching market_type to likely channels:

| Market Type | Primary Channels | Secondary Channels |
|---|---|---|
| B2C | landing-page, meta-ads, micro-influencer | reddit, word-of-mouth, seo-content |
| Prosumer | landing-page, community-participation, product-hunt | seo-content, twitter-dm, newsletter-sponsorship |
| B2B_SMB | landing-page, cold-email, seo-content | linkedin-dm, community-participation, podcast-guesting |
| B2B_Mid | cold-email, linkedin-dm, seo-content | newsletter-sponsorship, podcast-guesting, warm-intro |
| B2B_Enterprise | warm-intro, linkedin-dm, podcast-guesting | seo-content, cold-email (very targeted) |
| B2B2C | landing-page, cold-email, newsletter-sponsorship | meta-ads, seo-content |

## Buying Model Adjustments

| Buying Model | Channel Emphasis |
|---|---|
| SelfServe | Heavier on inbound, landing-page conversion matters most |
| SalesAssisted | Mix of inbound + light outbound, demo/call CTA |
| SalesLed | Heavy outbound, content supports sales conversations |
| Enterprise | Almost entirely outbound + referral, content builds authority |
