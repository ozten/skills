# ICP Patterns Reference

Common ICP segment patterns by vertical, plus decision trees for inferring market_type and buying_model.

## How to Use This File

When generating ICP hypotheses in Phase 2:
1. Identify the vertical(s) from the user's product description
2. Pull relevant segment templates from below
3. Adapt labels, pains, and watering holes to the specific product
4. Use the decision trees to infer market_type and buying_model

These are starting points, not rigid templates. Customize heavily based on the product's specific capabilities.

## Vertical: Real Estate / Property Management

### Typical Segments

**Mom & Pop Landlords (1-10 doors)**
- Pain: Manual tracking, tax season chaos, scattered spreadsheets
- Current alt: Excel, pen-and-paper, generic accounting software
- Triggers: Tax season, new property purchase, bad tenant experience
- Watering holes: BiggerPockets, local REIA meetups, Facebook groups
- Budget: Low ($10-50/mo)
- Decision maker: Individual

**Property Hustlers (11-50 doors)**
- Pain: Scaling operations without staff, maintenance coordination
- Current alt: AppFolio, Buildium, Rent Manager
- Triggers: Portfolio growth past comfort zone, hiring first employee
- Watering holes: BiggerPockets Pro, real estate podcasts, mastermind groups
- Budget: Mid ($50-200/mo)
- Decision maker: Individual

**Accidental Landlords**
- Pain: Didn't plan to be a landlord, overwhelmed by responsibilities
- Current alt: Property manager (high cost) or winging it
- Triggers: Inherited property, relocated but kept old home, divorce
- Watering holes: Reddit r/landlord, general personal finance forums
- Budget: Low ($10-50/mo)
- Decision maker: Individual or Household

**House Hackers**
- Pain: Balancing living-in with renting, tracking mixed-use expenses
- Current alt: Spreadsheets, guesswork
- Triggers: First property purchase, FHA loan closing
- Watering holes: BiggerPockets, FIRE community, house hacking podcasts
- Budget: Micro-Low (<$10-30/mo)
- Decision maker: Individual

**BRRRR Investors**
- Pain: Tracking rehab costs, managing refinance timelines, portfolio analytics
- Current alt: Custom spreadsheets, REI software
- Triggers: First flip, scaling past 2-3 properties
- Watering holes: BiggerPockets, real estate YouTube, investor meetups
- Budget: Mid ($50-200/mo)
- Decision maker: Individual

## Vertical: SaaS / Developer Tools

### Typical Segments

**Solo Developers / Indie Hackers**
- Pain: Wearing all hats, no time for marketing, shipping > selling
- Current alt: DIY everything, generic tools, nothing
- Triggers: First launch, first paying customer, hitting revenue plateau
- Watering holes: Indie Hackers, Hacker News, Twitter/X, dev Discord servers
- Budget: Low ($10-50/mo)
- Decision maker: Individual

**Small Dev Teams (2-10)**
- Pain: Coordination overhead, choosing the right tools, technical debt
- Current alt: Mix of open-source + cobbled-together SaaS
- Triggers: New team member, scaling past prototype, investor pressure
- Watering holes: GitHub, Stack Overflow, dev blogs, Slack communities
- Budget: Mid ($50-200/mo)
- Decision maker: Team

**DevOps / Platform Engineers**
- Pain: Infrastructure reliability, deployment speed, observability gaps
- Current alt: Datadog, PagerDuty, custom scripts
- Triggers: Production incident, team growth, compliance requirement
- Watering holes: DevOps subreddits, KubeCon, infrastructure blogs
- Budget: Mid-High ($100-500/mo)
- Decision maker: Team or Champion

**Technical Founders (Pre-PMF)**
- Pain: Building when they should be selling, unclear ICP, feature prioritization
- Current alt: Intuition, advisor advice, YC content
- Triggers: Running low on runway, first churn, fundraising prep
- Watering holes: YC forums, Lenny's Newsletter, First Round Review
- Budget: Low-Mid ($20-100/mo)
- Decision maker: Individual

## Vertical: Creator Economy

### Typical Segments

**Newsletter Operators**
- Pain: Monetization beyond ads, subscriber growth plateaus, automation
- Current alt: ConvertKit, Substack, Beehiiv
- Triggers: Hitting 1K/10K subscribers, first sponsorship inquiry
- Watering holes: Newsletter Twitter, Creator Economy podcasts, Beehiiv community
- Budget: Low-Mid ($20-100/mo)
- Decision maker: Individual

**Course Creators**
- Pain: Launch fatigue, student completion rates, pricing confidence
- Current alt: Teachable, Kajabi, Gumroad
- Triggers: Validating course idea, planning first launch, post-launch analysis
- Watering holes: Course creator communities, Pat Flynn, Ali Abdaal audience
- Budget: Mid ($50-200/mo)
- Decision maker: Individual

**YouTube / Video Creators**
- Pain: Ideation consistency, audience retention, sponsorship management
- Current alt: TubeBuddy, vidIQ, spreadsheets
- Triggers: Monetization threshold, brand deal inquiry, burnout
- Watering holes: YouTube creator forums, Twitter, VidSummit
- Budget: Low-Mid ($20-100/mo)
- Decision maker: Individual

## Vertical: E-commerce / DTC

### Typical Segments

**Shopify Solopreneurs**
- Pain: CAC too high, can't compete on ads, brand differentiation
- Current alt: Shopify apps, Facebook ads, influencer spray-and-pray
- Triggers: First $10K month, ad costs rising, inventory scaling
- Watering holes: Shopify community, ecom Twitter, My Wife Quit Her Job podcast
- Budget: Mid ($50-200/mo)
- Decision maker: Individual

**Etsy / Handmade Sellers**
- Pain: Platform dependency, margin compression, standing out in search
- Current alt: Etsy SEO guides, social media posting, hope
- Triggers: First 100 sales, Etsy fee increase, desire for own website
- Watering holes: Etsy seller forums, Reddit r/etsy, craft fairs
- Budget: Low ($10-50/mo)
- Decision maker: Individual

**DTC Brand Builders**
- Pain: Customer acquisition, retention, brand story, unit economics
- Current alt: Klaviyo, Triple Whale, agency
- Triggers: Scaling past $100K/yr, Series A, expanding to wholesale
- Watering holes: DTC Twitter, Modern Retail, Lean Luxe
- Budget: High ($200-1000/mo)
- Decision maker: Team

## Vertical: Local Business / SMB

### Typical Segments

**Solo Service Providers (plumber, tutor, trainer)**
- Pain: Feast-or-famine leads, no online presence, word-of-mouth only
- Current alt: Yelp, Google Business Profile, Nextdoor, nothing
- Triggers: Slow season, new competitor, desire to scale
- Watering holes: Local Facebook groups, trade associations, Nextdoor
- Budget: Low ($10-50/mo)
- Decision maker: Individual

**Small Agencies / Studios (2-10 people)**
- Pain: Client acquisition, project management, positioning
- Current alt: Clutch, referrals, LinkedIn outbound
- Triggers: Losing key client, hiring, niche-down decision
- Watering holes: Agency-specific Slack groups, industry conferences
- Budget: Mid ($50-200/mo)
- Decision maker: Team

**Online Fitness Coaches**
- Pain: Client management, programming delivery, marketing consistency
- Current alt: Trainerize, TrueCoach, spreadsheets + WhatsApp
- Triggers: Scaling past 20 clients, automating check-ins, raising prices
- Watering holes: Fitness business podcasts, Instagram, coach communities

- Budget: Mid ($50-200/mo)
- Decision maker: Individual

## Decision Trees

### Inferring MarketType

```
Is the end user paying personally?
├── Yes → Is the product for professional use?
│   ├── Yes → Prosumer
│   └── No  → B2C
└── No  → Does a company pay?
    ├── Yes → How many employees?
    │   ├── <50     → B2B_SMB
    │   ├── 50-500  → B2B_Mid
    │   └── 500+    → B2B_Enterprise
    └── No  → Does the buyer resell to consumers?
        ├── Yes → B2B2C
        └── No  → B2C (default)
```

### Inferring BuyingModel

```
What's the price point?
├── <$50/mo  → SelfServe (almost always)
├── $50-200  → Is the buyer technical?
│   ├── Yes → SelfServe
│   └── No  → SalesAssisted
├── $200-1K  → SalesAssisted
└── $1K+     → Is there procurement?
    ├── Yes → Enterprise
    └── No  → SalesLed
```

### Inferring DecisionMaker

```
MarketType?
├── B2C         → Individual (or Household for big purchases)
├── Prosumer    → Individual
├── B2B_SMB     → Individual or Team
├── B2B_Mid     → Champion or Team
├── B2B_Enterprise → Committee (Champion navigates)
└── B2B2C       → Team
```
