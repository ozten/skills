# Tone Archetypes Reference

Brand voice archetypes adapted for SaaS and indie products. Each archetype is a personality template — use them as starting points, then customize to the specific product and ICP.

## How to Use This File

1. In Phase 2, select 3-5 archetypes that *could* fit the brand positioning
2. In Phase 3, assign one archetype per treatment and develop it fully
3. Adapt the voice examples to the specific product — don't use the generic examples verbatim

---

## The Archetypes

### The Friendly Neighbor

**Core trait:** Warm, trustworthy, unpretentious
**Emotional register:** Calm confidence, gentle helpfulness
**Speaks like:** A friend who happens to know about this stuff

**Voice markers:**
- Conversational, uses contractions
- "You" centered — talks about the user's world, not the product
- Uses everyday analogies, not jargon
- Acknowledges the user's situation empathetically

**Example do/don't:**
| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| "No more digging through texts at tax time." | "Eliminate data retrieval inefficiencies." |
| "We built this because we had the same problem." | "Our solution leverages proprietary technology." |
| "Takes about 30 seconds to log a repair." | "Streamlined input workflow optimizes your time." |

**Best for ICP types:** Non-technical users, small business owners, first-time buyers, overwhelmed users
**Risky for:** Enterprise buyers (may feel unserious), technical audiences (may feel patronizing)

---

### The Straight Shooter

**Core trait:** Direct, no-bullshit, respects your time
**Emotional register:** Confidence without arrogance
**Speaks like:** A contractor who gives you the real estimate

**Voice markers:**
- Short sentences. Fragments OK.
- States facts, doesn't oversell
- Numbers and specifics over adjectives
- Zero filler words — no "just," "simply," "easily"

**Example do/don't:**
| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| "Log repairs. Track costs. Export for taxes." | "Our comprehensive solution helps you manage all aspects of..." |
| "Works on your phone. No app to download." | "Seamlessly accessible across all your devices." |
| "$12/month. Cancel anytime." | "Flexible pricing designed to scale with your needs." |

**Best for ICP types:** Time-poor professionals, action-oriented buyers, skeptical audiences, DIY types
**Risky for:** Users who want hand-holding, luxury/premium positioning

---

### The Savvy Insider

**Core trait:** Knowledgeable, slightly conspiratorial, "I know the game"
**Emotional register:** Smart confidence, shared insider knowledge
**Speaks like:** A friend in the industry giving you the real advice

**Voice markers:**
- Uses ICP-specific terminology naturally (not defined, just used)
- References real scenarios the ICP recognizes
- Slight "us vs them" energy — the user and brand vs. the problem
- Feels like it was written by someone who lives this

**Example do/don't:**
| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| "Your CPA wants a clean spreadsheet. We export one." | "Designed for professional accounting workflows." |
| "Stop paying AppFolio prices for a feature you need." | "A more affordable alternative to enterprise solutions." |
| "Every landlord has a shoebox. This replaces it." | "Document management solution for property owners." |

**Best for ICP types:** Niche professionals, communities with strong identity, prosumers
**Risky for:** Broad audiences (inside references exclude newcomers), beginners

---

### The Calm Authority

**Core trait:** Measured, competent, reassuring
**Emotional register:** Quiet confidence, "we've got this"
**Speaks like:** A trusted advisor who's seen it all

**Voice markers:**
- Complete, well-constructed sentences
- Explains *why* things work, not just *what* they do
- Uses "we" to create partnership feeling
- Measured pace — no urgency, no hype

**Example do/don't:**
| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| "Every repair gets logged with the date, cost, and property. When tax season comes, you're already done." | "NEVER MISS A DEDUCTION AGAIN!!!" |
| "We designed this for landlords managing 1-10 properties — the tools that exist are built for someone bigger." | "The #1 repair tracking solution disrupting property management!" |
| "Here's how it works." | "You won't BELIEVE how easy this is." |

**Best for ICP types:** Risk-averse buyers, users burned by bad software, professional contexts, considered purchases
**Risky for:** Young/energetic audiences, viral/social-first brands, impulse purchases

---

### The Playful Rebel

**Core trait:** Irreverent, energetic, slightly cheeky
**Emotional register:** Fun confidence, "we don't take ourselves too seriously"
**Speaks like:** A smart friend who makes you laugh while actually helping

**Voice markers:**
- Breaks conventions (unexpected metaphors, casual asides)
- Uses humor to disarm — especially about pain points
- Self-aware about being software ("yes, we're asking you to use an app for this")
- Emoji-friendly, meme-literate (when appropriate for ICP)

**Example do/don't:**
| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| "Your shoebox of receipts called. It wants a divorce." | "Eliminate paper-based record keeping." |
| "Built for the landlord who Googles 'do I need to track this?' at 11pm." | "Comprehensive compliance tracking for property owners." |
| "One less thing to panic about in April." | "Optimize your tax preparation workflow." |

**Best for ICP types:** Younger demographics, communities that value personality (Reddit, Twitter), anti-corporate audiences
**Risky for:** Older/conservative audiences, high-trust-required purchases, professional/enterprise contexts

---

### The Minimalist

**Core trait:** Restrained, precise, lets the product speak
**Emotional register:** Zen confidence, "less is more"
**Speaks like:** An Apple product page if it had a personality

**Voice markers:**
- Very few words — every word earns its place
- Whitespace IS the message (in copy layout too)
- Product-forward — describes what it does, not how you'll feel
- No exclamation marks. Period.

**Example do/don't:**
| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| "Track repairs. Know costs." | "The ultimate all-in-one repair tracking and cost management platform." |
| "Per property. Per repair. Per dollar." | "Comprehensive per-property cost breakdowns with detailed analytics." |
| "Simple Repair Tracker" | "SimpleRepairTracker.io — The Smart Way to Track Your Property Repairs" |

**Best for ICP types:** Design-conscious buyers, technical audiences, premium positioning
**Risky for:** Users who need hand-holding, audiences unfamiliar with the product category, low-price products (minimal copy can feel premium, which may conflict with budget positioning)

---

### The Empathetic Coach

**Core trait:** Understanding, encouraging, slightly motivational
**Emotional register:** Warm confidence, "I believe in you"
**Speaks like:** A mentor who's been where you are

**Voice markers:**
- Acknowledges the struggle before presenting the solution
- "You've got this" energy — empowering, not condescending
- Uses progressive disclosure — starts simple, reveals depth
- Celebrates small wins

**Example do/don't:**
| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| "Managing properties is hard enough. Tracking repairs shouldn't be." | "Reduce your operational burden with automated workflows." |
| "You started investing for freedom — not to drown in paperwork." | "Optimize your real estate portfolio management." |
| "Start with one property. Add more when you're ready." | "Unlimited properties on all plans!" |

**Best for ICP types:** Beginners, overwhelmed users, career-changers, accidental professionals
**Risky for:** Experienced professionals (may feel patronizing), action-oriented buyers (may feel slow)

## Archetype Selection Decision Tree

```
Is the ICP technically sophisticated?
├── Yes → Minimalist, Straight Shooter, or Savvy Insider
└── No  → Is the ICP anxious/overwhelmed?
    ├── Yes → Friendly Neighbor, Empathetic Coach, or Calm Authority
    └── No  → Is the ICP young / community-oriented?
        ├── Yes → Playful Rebel, Savvy Insider, or Straight Shooter
        └── No  → Friendly Neighbor, Calm Authority, or Straight Shooter
```

## Combining Archetypes

Treatments should be rooted in ONE primary archetype but can borrow a secondary trait:

**Compatible pairings:**
- Friendly Neighbor + Savvy Insider = "The helpful friend who knows the industry"
- Straight Shooter + Minimalist = "Blunt and stripped back"
- Calm Authority + Empathetic Coach = "The reassuring mentor"
- Playful Rebel + Savvy Insider = "The irreverent insider"

**Incompatible pairings:**
- Playful Rebel + Calm Authority (energy clash)
- Minimalist + Empathetic Coach (word count clash)
- Straight Shooter + Empathetic Coach (directness vs. softness)
